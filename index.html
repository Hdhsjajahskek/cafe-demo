Our Story

A small corner with a big heart

Ember & Oak began as a tiny espresso bar on Maple Street — a dream shared by two friends obsessed with the art of coffee. Six years later, it's a neighborhood institution: a warm, wood-filled sanctuary where locals, travelers, and remote workers all feel at home.

We source our beans directly from single-origin farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Each batch is roasted in-house every Tuesday and Friday, so every cup you drink is at its peak.
